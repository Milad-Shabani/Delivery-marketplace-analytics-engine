Sources & Methodology

This project uses synthetic data designed to simulate realistic food-delivery marketplace economics. It is not affiliated with or based on confidential or real data from any real company (Snapfood, DoorDash, Uber Eats, Deliveroo, or otherwise).

ComponentApproach
Data generationReproducible NumPy/pandas simulation (fixed seed) of ~12,000 customers, 320 restaurants, 520 riders, ~167k orders over 17 months, with seasonality, growth trend, churn/retention curves, and realistic financial relationships.
ForecastingSeasonal-naive, moving-average, and Holt-Winters models compared via rolling-origin (temporal) validation; best model selected per metric by WAPE.
OptimizationSciPy trust-constr solves a concave-response promotion-budget allocation across city × segment cells, maximizing incremental contribution profit under a fixed budget.
SegmentationRule-based on realized recency / frequency / monetary value and subscription status — not random labels.
